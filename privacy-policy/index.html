Privacy Policy & Sacred Confidentiality

Your trust and vulnerability are sacred to us. Here is our solemn commitment to honoring and protecting your personal details and healing journey.

Effective Date: August 2026 • Last Updated: August 2026

1. Our Commitment to Your Sacred Space

At The Art of Loving Academy and in the mentorship practice of Sireesha Puduru ("we", "our", or "us"), we believe that authentic transformation begins in a safe, non-judgmental, and strictly confidential environment.

This Privacy Policy governs the collection, use, and safeguarding of personal information provided through our website (sireeshapuduru.com), discovery call reservations, program registrations, and direct communication channels.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information that you voluntarily choose to provide when seeking guidance or engaging with our offerings:

  • Discovery Call Bookings: Your full name, email address, WhatsApp telephone number, preferred appointment slot, and primary area of healing focus.
  • Contact Inquiries: Your name, contact details, and the personal thoughts, challenges, or questions you choose to share in your message.
  • Free Meditation Gift: Your email address or WhatsApp number to transmit our guided audio meditation tracks and wisdom reflections.
  • Interactive Tools: Daily mirror-work affirmation draws and assessment responses are processed locally in your browser to maintain total privacy.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your details are used exclusively for the sacred purpose of nurturing your personal growth:

  • To schedule, confirm, and conduct your 20-minute Free Discovery Session.
  • To respond personally and compassionately to your messages and questions.
  • To deliver the free guided 432Hz meditation audio and workshop preparation guides.
  • To communicate crucial updates regarding the 21-Day Self-Love Challenge or the 2-Day Heal Your Life Intensive.

✦ We will never sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal data to any third-party advertisers or data brokers under any circumstances.

4. Practitioner-Client Confidentiality

All 1-to-1 mentorship conversations, emotional healing breakthroughs, past wounds, and personal vulnerabilities shared during sessions with Sireesha Puduru are held in the highest standard of spiritual and ethical confidentiality.

Any client testimonial or story featured on this platform is published only with the explicit, prior written consent of the participant, often with names anonymized to protect personal dignity.

5. Third-Party Integrations & Security

To deliver our services smoothly, we employ trusted, secure tools:

  • WhatsApp Integration: Direct messaging links communicate via WhatsApp's end-to-end encrypted infrastructure.
  • Web Audio API: Sound synthesizers generate 432Hz harmonic tones directly in your browser's audio engine without tracking your activity.
  • Encrypted Lead Storage: Form transmissions and records are safeguarded with industry-standard TLS encryption.

6. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You retain complete control over your personal data. At any time, you have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the contact information we hold on record.
  • Request the immediate and permanent deletion of your contact records.
  • Opt-out of any broadcast reminders or WhatsApp circle notifications.
